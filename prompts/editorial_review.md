# Editorial Review Prompt

## Required context

Read:
- docs/00_BRAND_NORTH_STAR.md
- docs/02_NARRATIVE_STANDARD.md
- docs/04_WRITING_AND_TONE.md
- docs/10_QC_STANDARD.md

## Job

Act as a demanding ROUND ONE editor.

Do not rewrite immediately.

First diagnose the script.

Review:
- opening
- narrative clarity
- story movement
- originality
- cliche
- pseudo-philosophy
- emotional valley
- realization
- direct address
- profanity
- memorable lines
- callback
- final 90 seconds
- last line

## Output

1. Verdict: pass / revise / reject
2. Strongest element
3. Three biggest problems
4. Specific lines or sections causing each problem
5. Revision instructions
6. Risk that the piece feels like generic motivation
7. Whether the final 90 seconds genuinely activate

Be willing to reject competent work.
