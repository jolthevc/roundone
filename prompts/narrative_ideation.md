## Job

Generate concepts, not scripts.

Each concept must contain:
- working title
- core realization
- narrative archetype
- story vehicle
- opening image
- central tension
- emotional valley
- reversal
- ignition
- possible callback
- factual research needs
- why this belongs in ROUND ONE

## Standard

Prefer one clean idea over a clever pile of ideas.

Reject your own concept if:
- it sounds like generic motivation
- the metaphor is obvious and stale
- the story is only an excuse for advice
- the ending has no natural callback
- there is no emotional movement
- it duplicates a recent theme

Generate fewer strong concepts rather than many filler concepts.
