# Narrative Treatment Prompt

## Required context

Read:
- docs/00_BRAND_NORTH_STAR.md
- docs/02_NARRATIVE_STANDARD.md
- docs/03_NARRATIVE_ARCHETYPES.md
- docs/04_WRITING_AND_TONE.md

## Input

An approved narrative concept.

## Job

Create a beat sheet for a 5 to 7 minute Round.

Map:
- opening 0:00 to 0:30
- story development
- tension increase
- valley
- realization
- turn toward listener
- final 60 to 90 second fire
- callback / last line

For each beat include:
- what happens
- what the listener should feel
- what information is revealed
- what must not be explained yet

## Rule

Do not write the final prose.

Solve structure before style.

## Self-check

Confirm:
- one core idea
- concrete opening
- clear movement
- earned realization
- final act grows from the story
- no dead middle
