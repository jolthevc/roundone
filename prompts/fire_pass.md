# Fire Pass Prompt

## Required context

Read:
- docs/00_BRAND_NORTH_STAR.md
- docs/02_NARRATIVE_STANDARD.md
- docs/04_WRITING_AND_TONE.md
- docs/10_QC_STANDARD.md

## Job

Review only the final act of an otherwise approved script.

The question is:

**Does this fucking move someone?**

Evaluate:
- transition into direct address
- rhythm
- sentence compression
- force
- profanity
- repetition
- behavioral verbs
- callback
- final line
- emotional truth

## Rules

Do not add aggression merely to increase volume.

Do not attack worth.

Do not turn the ending into a detachable motivational speech.

The ending must belong to the story that came before it.

## Output

1. Fire score 1 to 10
2. What lands
3. What feels fake
4. Where momentum drops
5. Exact revision plan
6. Proposed revised final act only if revision is needed
