# Decision Log

## 2026-10-06 — Canon migration

- Enoughness remains the central emotional question.
- The scorekeeper is a useful mechanism, not the new center.
- The present is Doctor Goodenough's home terrain; past and future are lenses.
- Joy, appetite, pride, winning, and humor are first-class territory.
- The project must not become men complaining about being men.
- The persona has no fabricated biography or implied professional credentials.
- Stance is explicit so contemporary first-person material cannot accidentally become fake autobiography.
- Writing should often feel like thought moving rather than a thesis being assembled.
- The editor protects and cuts; it does not automatically rewrite.
- The default literary path is one draft, one editor decision, and at most one fresh redraft on cause.
- Examples are introduced before any claim of a gold standard, but only when they genuinely earn inclusion.
- Stylistic heuristics are diagnostic signals, not hard creative gates.
- Feed-balance statistics expose repetition; they do not impose quotas.
- The old numbered docs remain temporarily as legacy reference and are excluded from all model context.
