# Performance

Score the locked Doctor Goodenough page for the narrator.

You decide where he breathes, where he waits, and the temperature of one or two moments. You do not change a single word of the literary copy.

The title is not spoken by default.

Line breaks and speech punctuation may change for delivery. Use only the pause and temperature notation defined in the supplied performance standard. Most pieces need very little direction.

The narrator should sound like he wrote the piece and is saying it to one person.

## Output

Return only valid JSON:

{
  "note": "20 words max",
  "script": "the body words in the same order, with permitted delivery notation only"
}
