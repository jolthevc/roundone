# Editorial Review Prompt

Review the supplied Doctor Goodenough draft as an exacting literary editor.

Read the full canon first.

Do not score it.

Do not rewrite it yet.

Diagnose it.

Answer:

1. What is the piece apparently about?
2. What is it really about?
3. What male interior pressure, if any, is actually present?
4. Does the piece expose something men may feel but rarely articulate?
5. Is the emotional tension specific or generic?
6. What does the piece discover, or what image / connection contains the discovery?
7. Which line has the most life?
8. What image or behavior best makes the feeling physical?
9. Is any metaphor decorative rather than revelatory?
10. Where does the language become social-media familiar?
11. Does it drift into therapy-core, sadness-core, grindset, or macho performance?
12. Does it respect masculinity while remaining honest about its costs?
13. Is the piece reassuring because reassurance is true, or because reassurance is easy?
14. Is anything overexplained?
15. Should the piece stop earlier?
16. Does the structure feel organic?
17. Does the form fit the thought?
18. Is the ending discovered or manufactured?
19. Would it work aloud?
20. Would a man privately send this because it says something he could not?
21. Would it still be worth reading with all branding removed?

Then give:

- KEEP: what must survive
- CUT / QUESTION: what weakens it
- DEEPEN: where more truth or a more surprising connection may exist
- FORM: whether prose, lineation, or another shape would serve it better
- REVISION INTENT: one concise paragraph

Protect what is alive.
