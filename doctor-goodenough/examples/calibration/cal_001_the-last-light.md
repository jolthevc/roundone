# THE LAST LIGHT

When I was a boy, someone always told me when it was time to come inside.

From the yard. From the street. From the dark, when I'd stayed out too long.

Now I turn off every light in this house. I check the locks. I make sure everyone is asleep.

Some nights I still wait to hear my name.
