# 09 — Visual Standard

## The page

Doctor Goodenough should feel like a page from a dark literary book that somehow appeared inside a social feed.

The page is an editorial object, not a quote card.

## What must be consistent

The visual system should be highly consistent from piece to piece.

Lock:

- 9:16 canvas
- near-black paper tone
- paper / print texture
- title font
- title size
- title color
- title tracking
- title horizontal position
- title vertical position
- body font
- body color
- body width
- body left edge
- signature style
- signature anchor
- illustration style
- overall visual temperature

The title must not move because one narrative is longer or shorter.

The typography should not change to "fit the mood."

Consistency creates the world.

## What may adapt

The writing is alive, so the page needs controlled flexibility.

The following may adapt within defined bounds:

- paragraph breaks
- lineation
- amount of white space
- body length
- body font size within a narrow range
- body block height
- illustration subject
- illustration scale within a narrow range
- illustration exact composition

The system should accommodate prose, lineated prose, and poem-like structures without making them look like different brands.

## Title

The title appears in the same position every time.

It should be small, understated, and editorial.

It should invite rather than explain.

Avoid clickbait, theme labels, and motivational headings.

## Body

The full piece should normally be visible from the first frame.

The body should feel like typeset literature.

Do not shrink the type until readability collapses.

If the writing does not fit the visual object gracefully, reconsider the writing.

## Signature

Use:

**— Doctor Goodenough**

in a consistent signature treatment near the bottom-left.

It should feel authored, not corporate.

## Illustration

Every finished page should normally contain one small hand-drawn / etched illustration near the bottom.

The **style is fixed**.

The **subject is narrative-dependent**.

The illustration should emerge from the piece itself.

A piece about childhood might use a bicycle, porch light, mask, kitchen window, or plastic candy bucket.

A piece about fatherhood might use hands, a tool bench, a chair, a car, or another emotionally relevant object.

Do not default to the same moon, mountain, or shoreline.

The image is a visual echo of the writing, not generic atmosphere.

### Illustration style

- ivory / warm-white linework
- hand-drawn
- etched / inked
- slightly imperfect
- literary
- restrained
- detailed enough to reward looking
- never photoreal
- never cartoonish
- never glossy

## Blank master

The blank master establishes:

- exact background
- exact texture
- title anchor
- body safe area
- signature anchor
- illustration safe area

It is a production reference, not a content template.

## Motion

Motion is almost absent.

Preferred:

- very slight push-in
- faint paper movement
- subtle grain
- tiny luminance drift

Avoid typing animations, kinetic captions, flashy transitions, page flips, and decorative AI motion.

Stillness is part of the premium feeling.

## The visual test

If the audio were muted, would this look like a page someone might save because the writing itself appears worth reading?
