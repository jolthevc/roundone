# 09 — Visual Standard

## The page

Doctor Goodenough should feel like a page from a dark literary book that somehow appeared inside a social feed.

The page is not content design.

It is an editorial object.

## Canonical direction

- 9:16 vertical frame
- near-black or black background
- warm white / ivory typography
- subtle paper or print texture
- classic readable serif body type
- generous margins
- strong but quiet hierarchy
- no decorative clutter
- no giant social-media quote typography
- no kinetic-caption aesthetic

## Title

The title belongs at the top.

Do not place ROUND ONE as the main header on the page.

Titles should normally be short, literary, and understated.

They should invite rather than explain.

Avoid clickbait, self-help framing, or generic theme labels.

## Body

The full literary piece is normally visible from the first frame.

It should look readable, not dense.

Line length, paragraph breaks, and whitespace should make the page feel like a book rather than a screenshot of an essay.

Do not shrink the type merely to force an overlong piece onto one page.

If the page becomes visually punishing, the writing or format needs reconsideration.

## Signature

The bottom of the page carries the Doctor Goodenough signature.

This may include:
- the name "Doctor Goodenough"
- a consistent handwritten or scribbled signature treatment
- a small recurring scribbled icon or mark

The signature should feel personal, not like a corporate footer.

## Brand relationship

ROUND ONE can remain present through account identity, captioning, metadata, profile, or a very restrained secondary mark if later needed.

The page itself should prioritize the title, writing, and Doctor Goodenough.

## Motion

Motion is almost absent.

Preferred:
- very slight push-in
- faint texture movement
- subtle grain
- tiny luminance drift

Avoid:
- typing animations
- kinetic captions
- flashy transitions
- unnecessary page flips
- decorative AI motion

Stillness is part of the premium feeling.

## Color

Default:
- black / near-black
- warm white / ivory

A tiny restrained accent may be tested later, but the default system should work without one.

## The visual test

If the audio were muted, would this look like a page someone might save because the writing itself appears worth reading?
