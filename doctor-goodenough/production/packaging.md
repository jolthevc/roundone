# Packaging

Packaging happens only after literary copy is locked.

## Illustration subject

Choose one small object or place adjacent to the piece's world rather than simply drawing its central noun. Objects and places are preferred. A hand may appear when genuinely useful. Avoid faces, full figures, text, logos, and brands.

Do not repeat the same illustration subject frequently across recent posts.

The image-generation prompt should be assembled by code from:
1. the fixed Doctor Goodenough illustration style
2. the chosen subject
3. a short composition note

The model selecting the subject does not invent the visual style.

## Caption

A caption is optional.

When present, it should be plain, short, and secondary to the piece. It may add a little context. It never summarizes the writing, quotes its best line, asks a question, or asks the reader to save, share, follow, comment, or tag.

Do not depend on hashtags for identity. If hashtags are used, keep a tiny fixed set rather than generating them per post.

## Alt text

Alt text should state that the post is a page of text, give the title, and describe the small illustration plainly.
