# Voice

## Narrator

Doctor Goodenough is voiced by an American man in his early sixties.

The voice is deep, weathered, and refined: low pitch, gently gravelly, with a slight rasp beneath an otherwise smooth delivery. He is close to the microphone, quiet, unhurried, warm, dry, and faintly world-weary. There may be sadness in the grain of the voice, but never defeat.

He sounds like he wrote what he is reading and is saying it to one person.

Avoid preacher, professor, audiobook, movie-trailer, therapeutic, and motivational-speaker delivery. Do not boom. Do not overact.

Core feeling: **a refined man who has been through enough to speak softly.**

## Casting rule

Doctor Goodenough has one fixed production voice. Once selected, the voice ID and provider/model version should remain stable unless a deliberate recast is approved.

The Doctor Goodenough voice must not reuse a ROUND ONE voice ID. ROUND ONE's Elder and Doctor Goodenough should be distinguishable immediately.

## Voice-design prompt

Doctor Goodenough is a man in his early 60s with a deep, weathered, refined voice. Low pitch, gently gravelly, with a worn texture beneath an otherwise smooth delivery. He sounds handsome without trying to sound handsome, like someone who once had effortless polish but life has roughed up the edges.

Speak intimately and quietly, as if reading something personal to one person late at night. Close-mic, natural breath, slight rasp, occasional hesitation. Slow and deliberate, but never sleepy. Let silence do some of the work.

He is intelligent, understated, dry, warm, and faintly world-weary. There is sadness in the grain of the voice, but not defeat.

Avoid preacher, professor, audiobook, movie-trailer, or motivational-speaker delivery. No booming bass or overacting.

Core feeling: a refined man who has been through enough to speak softly.
