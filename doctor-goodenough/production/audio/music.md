# Music

Doctor Goodenough uses **three canonical music beds**.

That is intentional.

The goal is not to score each post with a new soundtrack. The goal is to build a recognizable sonic room around the voice.

All three tracks should feel like variations inside the same house: nocturnal, elegant, restrained, intimate, slightly mysterious, and emotionally warm. The differences between them should be subtle enough that a listener recognizes Doctor Goodenough before recognizing which track is playing.

## Shared sound

Soft analog synths, distant felt piano, low warm strings, faint glassy texture, subtle tape haze, occasional high airy tones. Slow and spacious. No obvious beat, no drums, no drop, no cinematic climax, no inspirational crescendo.

The voice remains the foreground. Music is the room around it.

Core composition instruction:

> **Do not compose a story. Compose an emotional state.**

A random thirty-second window should work beneath almost any Doctor Goodenough piece.

## Selection

Do not use an LLM to choose music.

Default behavior is simple rotation across the three approved tracks, skipping an immediate repeat. The console may allow a manual override before publication.

Do not build music families, probabilistic routing, or per-piece generative music unless real published output later proves the three-track system insufficient.

## One-time creation brief

Elegant nocturnal ambient instrumental. No vocals, spoken word, or chanting. Soft analog synths, distant felt piano, low warm strings, faint glassy textures, subtle tape haze, and occasional high airy tones. Slow, spacious, refined, slightly melancholic but still hopeful. Gentle repetition, minimal harmony, no obvious beat, no drums, no crescendo, no cinematic climax. Sophisticated, intimate, literary, and emotionally warm. Enough melodic identity to be memorable, restrained enough to sit underneath narration.

Create three tracks that clearly belong to the same sonic identity rather than three different genres.
