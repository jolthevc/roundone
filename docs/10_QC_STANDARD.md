# 10 - QC Standard

ROUND ONE should be difficult to approve.

Volume is not the goal.

The catalog compounds only if the average quality stays high.

## Approval philosophy

A Round is not approved because:
- it is competent
- the grammar is clean
- the AI completed the task
- the story technically fits the format
- the ending sounds motivational

A Round is approved because it works as media and changes state.

## Stage gates

### Gate 1 - Concept

Pass only if:
- one clear idea
- one compelling story vehicle
- emotional movement is plausible
- ending opportunity exists
- no obvious cliche at the center

### Gate 2 - Treatment

Pass only if:
- opening scene is concrete
- tension increases
- valley exists where appropriate
- realization is earned
- callback or ending mechanism is visible
- no structural dead zone

### Gate 3 - Script

Score:
- opening strength
- narrative clarity
- originality
- emotional movement
- specificity
- memorable lines
- realization
- ending
- tone
- cliche risk

### Gate 4 - Fire Pass

Evaluate the final 60 to 90 seconds separately.

Ask:
- does the energy actually rise
- does direct address feel earned
- does profanity help
- are verbs strong
- does the callback land
- is there one final line worth silence
- would this make someone more likely to move

### Gate 5 - Factual Integrity

For factual pieces:
- facts sourced
- quotes verified
- chronology checked
- no invented dialogue presented as fact
- no misleading compression
- names and pronunciations checked

### Gate 6 - Performance

Check:
- human cadence
- emotional credibility
- pacing
- pauses
- pronunciation
- dynamic range
- no overacting
- no synthetic rhythm

### Gate 7 - Music

Check:
- voice remains dominant
- bed is steady
- no accidental climax
- no emotional contradiction
- no distracting motif
- mix is clear

### Gate 8 - Visual

Check:
- one clear symbol
- correct style
- negative space
- no hyperreal AI look
- not too grungy
- not cliche
- no unwanted text
- title remains legible when layered by product

### Gate 9 - Master

Listen end to end.

Do not approve by reviewing isolated components only.

## Hard-fail conditions

Reject the Round if:
- the metaphor requires explanation
- the core idea is generic
- factual material is invented or uncertain
- the narrator attacks the listener's worth
- the script sounds like a motivational poster
- profanity is unearned
- the final act is detachable generic hype
- the music performs a dramatic arc
- the visual looks like generic AI inspiration art
- the piece is boring for more than a short intentional pause
- the listener finishes with less agency

## Scoring model

Use a 1 to 10 scale for:
- Concept
- Opening
- Story
- Emotional Movement
- Originality
- Realization
- Memorable Line
- Final 90 Seconds
- Performance
- Music Fit
- Visual Fit
- Overall Activation

Do not average blindly.

Some categories are gates.

A Round with great visuals, great voice, and weak realization is still weak.

## Suggested thresholds

Working standard:
- no core narrative score below 7
- Final 90 Seconds should usually be 8+
- Overall Activation should usually be 8+
- factual integrity is pass/fail

These thresholds will be recalibrated after gold standards exist.

## Rejection library

Keep a small curated set of rejected examples with notes.

Useful rejection categories:
- too philosophical
- too cliche
- weak opening
- overexplained metaphor
- fake intensity
- weak final act
- music too active
- visual too literal
- visual too grungy

The point is not to archive every failure.

The point is to teach future creators what ROUND ONE refuses to become.
