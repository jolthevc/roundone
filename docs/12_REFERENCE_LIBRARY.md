# 12 - Reference Library

References exist to identify transferable creative principles.

They are not permission to imitate protected works, recognizable voices, melodies, compositions, scripts, or visual assets.

For every reference, ask:

**What behavior are we learning from this?**

## Narrative and advertising references

### Derrick Rose - Wake Up
Useful for:
- emotional compression
- seriousness
- identity
- tension
- letting a simple premise carry weight

### Powerade - Rose From Concrete
Useful for:
- one metaphor
- toughness without constant explanation
- personal story connecting to a broader truth

### Jimmy Graham - Churn
Useful for:
- process
- repetition
- physical detail
- converting unseen work into emotional meaning

### Nike - Find Your Greatness
Useful for:
- widening the frame
- human-scale opening
- universal meaning emerging from one simple image

### Under Armour - Rule Yourself
Useful for:
- repetition
- accumulated unseen work
- disciplined visual and narrative economy

### Michael Jordan - Failure
Useful for:
- reversal
- failure as evidence of participation
- directness

### Serena - Voice of Belief
Useful for:
- identity
- conviction
- internal voice

### Guinness wheelchair basketball
Useful for:
- reveal
- reframing
- meaning changing after the listener understands the setup

## Music references

Learn:
- hypnotic repetition
- unresolved tension
- intensity without crescendo

### do i clench my fists? - ridgeclub
Learn:

- processed human texture

- quiet pressure

### Daredevil theme
Learn:
- pulse
- weight
- dark seriousness
- low repeating motif

### Red Sex - Vessel
Learn selectively:
- physical menace
- industrial texture
- pressure

Use much less foreground activity.

### Jacob and the Stone - Emile Mosseri
Learn:
- beautiful weight
- emotional seriousness
- transcendence without obvious triumph

### Je te laisserai des mots
Learn:
- intimacy
- simplicity
- tenderness
- restraint

### Another Love - instrumental reference
Learn:
- ache
- yearning
- emotional directness

### On the Nature of Daylight - Max Richter
Learn:
- sustained gravity
- emotional weight

This is near the upper boundary of how directive the soundtrack should become.

### Ominous reference clip
Learn:
- steady dark atmosphere
- inward-looking mood
- emotional ambiguity
- usefulness beneath unrelated narration

## Visual references

The canonical visual reference currently is the approved rose-through-concrete direction developed during brand exploration.

Learn:
- one object
- lower-center placement
- huge black negative space
- painted treatment
- restrained burgundy accent
- soft local light
- dark fine-art editorial quality
- less realism
- restrained texture

Avoid reproducing any reference artwork directly.

## Reference rule

A future creator should be able to explain:
1. what principle was borrowed
2. what surface elements were deliberately not copied
3. why the resulting work is distinctly ROUND ONE
