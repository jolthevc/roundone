# 13 - Creative Assembly Standard

A Round is not four separate assets.

Narrative, voice, music, and visual must feel like one world.

The goal is cohesion without redundancy.

## The four pillars

1. Narrative carries meaning.
2. Voice carries emotional movement.
3. Music carries atmosphere and weight.
4. Visual carries identity and symbolic grounding.

Each pillar has a job.

Problems begin when one pillar tries to do another pillar's job.

## Narrative and voice

The script should create the arc on the page.

The voice should reveal that arc in performance.

Do not write a flat script and ask performance to rescue it.

Do not over-direct a script whose language already contains the rhythm.

## Voice and music

The narrator is always foreground.

The music should support intimacy, pressure, gravity, and silence.

The music should not tell the narrator when to become emotional.

If the voice becomes quiet, the music does not need to become quiet.
If the voice becomes loud, the music does not need to swell.

That independence is a feature.

## Narrative and visual

The visual should anchor the story with one symbol.

It should not summarize the plot.

A stone story can use a split stone.
A regret story may use a chair, clock, match, or door.

The object should carry enough connection to feel intentional, but enough abstraction to preserve imagination.

## Music and visual

Music and visual should share emotional register without matching literally.

A dark visual does not require the darkest soundtrack.
A burgundy object does not require warm music.
A visual with a match does not require crackling-fire sound design.

Avoid obvious sensory doubling.

## Restraint

When in doubt, remove.

Do not add a second visual object, second narrator, second music cue, sound effects, orchestral punctuation, or kinetic typography unless the piece demonstrably improves.

## Integration test

Before approval, ask:
- If the music is muted, does the narrative still work?
- If the image is hidden, does the audio still work?
- If the voice is isolated, does the emotional arc remain?
- If the title is removed, does the image still feel like ROUND ONE?
- Does any asset feel like it is begging for attention?

The best assembly feels inevitable and simple.
