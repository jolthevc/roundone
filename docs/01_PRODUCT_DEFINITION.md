# 01 - Product Definition

## Canonical product

The canonical ROUND ONE product is audio.

A Round is typically 5 to 7 minutes long and consists of:

1. one narrative
2. one narrator
3. one continuous music bed
4. one symbolic cover image
5. one finished audio master

The listener should be able to press play, put the phone down, and receive the complete experience.

If the phone locks five seconds after playback begins, nothing essential should be lost.

## Audio first

Audio is not an export format for a video product.

Audio is the product.

The visual exists to:
- create a premium frame
- give the Round identity
- establish mood before playback
- support library browsing
- support social derivatives

The visual is not required to explain the story.

## The daily behavior

The ideal Round can fit naturally into:
- getting dressed
- showering
- brushing teeth
- walking
- driving
- commuting
- gym warmup
- sitting alone before work
- preparing for a difficult conversation
- resetting after a bad period

The product should feel more like a ritual than a task.

## Content unit

Working term: **Round**

Examples:
- Today's Round
- Your Round is ready
- Save this Round
- Replay this Round

Do not overuse boxing vocabulary elsewhere.

The brand metaphor should remain buried enough that the product does not become a boxing-themed app.

## Delivery principle

The experience should be easy to enter.

The long-term delivery mechanism may include messaging, app notifications, email, or another channel. That infrastructure is not defined in this creative phase.

The desired consumer feeling is:

**Today's Round is ready. Press play.**

No setup burden.
No lesson plan.
No journaling requirement.
No mandatory response.

## Personalization philosophy

Personalization should primarily operate at the level of:
- identity
- aspiration
- emotional preference
- life stage
- tolerance for aggression
- tolerance for profanity
- preferred voice
- preferred narrative register
- listening history

Avoid invasive operational personalization.

ROUND ONE should not sound like:
You have a deck due at 3 PM and a dentist appointment at 5.

It should sound like the right piece found the right person at the right moment.

## Library model

ROUND ONE should build a proprietary catalog of excellent finished masters.

The preferred model is:
- create excellent canonical Rounds
- tag them deeply
- program them intelligently
- learn what resonates
- compound the library over time

The default should not be generating a unique piece for every individual every day.

Quality and reusability beat infinite novelty.

## One piece, one world

Each Round should generally use:
- one narrator
- one continuous music bed
- one symbolic image

Do not change soundtrack halfway through to manufacture emotion.

Do not create a visual montage.

Do not turn a Round into a sequence of cinematic scenes.

Restraint is part of the product quality.

## Social derivatives

Social video may use:
- the cover image
- subtle animated atmosphere
- captions
- selected excerpts
- audio waveform treatment if tasteful

Social derivatives are distribution assets, not the canonical Round.

**Doctor Goodenough is a separate creative property, not a ROUND ONE social derivative. Do not cut Rounds into Doctor Goodenough pieces or expand Doctor Goodenough pieces into Rounds by default. Its canon lives under `doctor-goodenough/` and is independently scoped.**

## Product experience principles

The player should feel:
- dark
- simple
- immediate
- premium
- uncluttered

The creative asset should dominate.

Avoid:
- gamification clutter
- streak anxiety as the primary retention tool
- bright self-help aesthetics
- excessive controls
- feeds that feel like generic content consumption

## What remains intentionally unresolved

The following are deferred for the ROUND ONE flagship:
- app versus web architecture
- SMS/RCS/iMessage-style delivery implementation
- authentication
- subscription billing
- storage
- orchestration
- personalization engine
- recommendation algorithm
- production console
- API providers
- queueing
- workflow automation
- asset pipelines

Those decisions should follow the creative canon, not determine it.
