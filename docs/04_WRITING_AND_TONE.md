# 04 - Writing and Tone

ROUND ONE writing should sound spoken before it sounds written.

The listener should feel that a human voice is talking to them, not reading an essay at them.

## Core qualities

The writing should be:
- conversational
- clear
- specific
- rhythmic
- emotionally intelligent
- forceful when earned
- simple enough to follow while moving
- sophisticated without sounding academic

## Sentence behavior

Early story sections can use:
- longer sentences
- sensory detail
- pacing
- silence
- uncertainty

As intensity rises:
- sentences often shorten
- verbs get stronger
- repetition can increase
- abstraction should decrease
- direct address can increase

The writing should physically support performance.

## Accessible intelligence

Never dumb the listener down.

Also never make the listener work unnecessarily hard to decode the point.

Prefer:
The stone did not split because of the ninety-ninth hit alone.

Over:
The terminal rupture was merely the visible culmination of accumulated invisible force.

## Masculinity

ROUND ONE can feel masculine without performing masculinity.

Good masculine energy:
- responsibility
- restraint
- courage
- reliability
- willingness to act
- willingness to feel pain without becoming defined by it
- protecting one's own dignity and the dignity of others

Bad masculine energy:
- dominance theater
- contempt
- chest-beating
- humiliation
- treating emotion as weakness
- reducing life to winning status competitions

## Aggression

Aggression is a tool.

It should be aimed at:
- hesitation
- excuses
- avoidance
- passivity
- self-deception
- wasted opportunity

It should not be aimed at:
- inherent worth
- body
- intelligence
- masculinity
- social status
- mental health
- other vulnerable traits

Good:
Stop waiting for permission.

Good:
You've been fucking around.

Bad:
You're a loser.

Bad:
Real men would have done it already.

## Profanity

Profanity is permitted.

It should feel:
- earned
- rhythmic
- intentional
- emotionally useful

It should not feel:
- constant
- juvenile
- decorative
- algorithmically inserted

A clean line can hit harder than a profane line.
A profane line should exist because the line needs it.

## Direct address

You is powerful and dangerous.

Use it after the story has earned intimacy.

Do not begin every Round by immediately diagnosing the listener.

Avoid:
You know exactly what you've been doing wrong.

Prefer:
Let the listener recognize themselves first.

Then speak directly.

## Confidence

The narrator should sound convicted.

The narrator should not sound omniscient.

Avoid:
- guru certainty
- promises of inevitable success
- everything happens for a reason
- telling the listener what their life definitely means
- pretending suffering guarantees reward

We can say:
This may still fail. Go anyway.

That is stronger than false certainty.

## Philosophy

Philosophy is allowed when grounded.

The preferred sequence:
1. concrete event
2. observation
3. implication
4. principle

Not:
1. abstract principle
2. abstract principle
3. metaphor
4. more abstract principle

## Metaphor

Use one strong metaphor rather than five.

If the Round is about a stone, let the stone remain the dominant symbolic system.

Do not add storms, oceans, mountains, fire, war, and roads unless the story actually needs them.

Mixed metaphors make the piece feel written rather than lived.

## Emotional honesty

Do not turn every pain into a hidden gift.

Sometimes:
- failure was failure
- time was wasted
- someone left
- the opportunity is gone
- the listener made the wrong choice

The power comes from what remains possible now.

Not from pretending the past was secretly perfect.

## Cliche detector

Reject or rewrite lines that sound like:
- motivational wallpaper
- LinkedIn inspiration
- gym captions
- wellness slogans
- sports commercial parody
- pseudo-Stoicism
- internet masculinity discourse

If a line could be printed over a lion photo without changing it, inspect it carefully.

## Humor

Humor is allowed.

Dry humor, human observation, and slight irreverence can make a Round more credible.

Do not force jokes into emotional moments.

## Ending language

The final act can become more direct.

Possible behaviors:
- commands
- repetition
- second-person address
- profanity
- faster rhythm
- callbacks
- short declarative sentences

But the ending should still sound like the same piece.

## Tone test

A ROUND ONE script should feel like it was written by someone who:
- respects the listener
- has lived enough to know certainty is rare
- is willing to say hard things
- does not need to humiliate anyone
- believes action matters
- is not embarrassed by emotion
- is allergic to bullshit
