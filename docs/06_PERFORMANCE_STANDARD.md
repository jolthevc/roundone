# 06 - Performance Standard

A strong script can still fail in performance.

ROUND ONE performance should feel authored sentence by sentence.

## Governing principle

**The narrator creates the emotional arc.**

Music provides atmosphere and weight.
The narrator creates:
- tension
- vulnerability
- intimacy
- acceleration
- confrontation
- release

## Performance dimensions

Every performance can be directed across:
- pace
- volume
- energy
- distance
- breath
- pause
- emphasis
- emotional temperature
- certainty
- trajectory

These dimensions are more useful than vague instructions such as make it inspiring.

## Performance script

The clean editorial script and performance script are separate artifacts.

The editorial script optimizes language.

The performance script may include cues such as:
- quiet
- closer
- slower
- hold
- pause 1s
- pause 2s
- harder
- building
- smile in voice
- almost a whisper
- raised voice
- break
- let this land

Use cues sparingly.

If every line has a tag, direction has become noise.

## Opening performance

Default:
- intimate
- grounded
- no immediate yelling
- enough space for curiosity
- trust the story

The listener should lean in.

## Mid-piece performance

The middle can widen:
- more certainty
- slightly faster pace
- clearer emphasis
- increased emotional proximity

But preserve dynamic range for the end.

## Final act

The final 60 to 90 seconds may:
- shorten pauses
- increase pace
- bring the voice closer
- increase volume selectively
- add sharper emphasis
- permit shouting where earned
- use silence immediately after a peak

Do not make the entire final minute one continuous shout.

Contrast creates force.

## Whispering

Whispering can be powerful when:
- the line is intimate
- the music already carries pressure
- the narrator is saying something the listener must confront
- a loud moment has just ended

Avoid theatrical whispering.

## Shouting

Shouting is rare.

Use it when the script has earned rupture.

A shout should feel like pressure finally breaking through, not like the default persona.

## Pauses

Pauses are content.

Use pauses to:
- let an image form
- let a realization land
- create discomfort
- reset after intensity
- isolate the final line

Do not use identical mechanical pauses after every sentence.

## Breath

Natural breath increases humanity.

Do not over-clean the performance until it feels synthetic.

Breath can also communicate:
- exhaustion
- intimacy
- restraint
- urgency

But audible breathing should never become distracting.

## Emphasis

Prefer emphasis through:
- timing
- pace
- silence
- sentence shape

before simply increasing loudness.

## Pronunciation

Proper nouns, technical terms, foreign words, and uncommon places must be reviewed.

Do not accept a beautiful performance with distracting mispronunciations.

## Performance QC

Listen for:
- AI sing-song cadence
- repeated sentence melody
- unnatural emphasis
- swallowed endings
- abrupt energy shifts
- fake emotion
- overacting
- clipping
- awkward pauses
- pronunciation errors
- insufficient dynamic range

## Final test

Mute the music.

Does the narration still create an arc?

If not, the performance is relying on the soundtrack to do its job.
